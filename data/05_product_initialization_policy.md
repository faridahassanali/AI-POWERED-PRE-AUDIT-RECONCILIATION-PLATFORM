# Wallet Product Initialization Procedure
Policy ID: WALLET-POLICY-001
Version: 1.0 (Synthetic)

## Process
1. Receive the partner customer file.
2. Validate mandatory customer fields and establish the approved source population.
3. Complete screening and risk checks.
4. Validate Arabic name requirements.
5. Identify dormant accounts and apply required handling.
6. Only then proceed with wallet initialization for eligible customers.
7. Produce a final initialization/audit report.

## Control Principle
The final report must reflect the approved source population and underlying source/processing results accurately.
