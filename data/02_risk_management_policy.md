# Customer Risk Management Policy
Policy ID: RISK-POLICY-001
Version: 1.0 (Synthetic)

## Requirements
1. Each customer must have a risk level recorded.
2. High-risk customers require enhanced review before product activation.
3. A high-risk customer must not be treated as eligible for standard wallet activation unless an approved exception is recorded.
4. An approved exception must have an exception reference and reviewer recorded.

## Audit Evidence
Customer ID, risk level, decision/exception, exception reference, reviewer, and activation status should be available.
