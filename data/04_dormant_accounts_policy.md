# Dormant Account Handling Policy
Policy ID: DORMANT-POLICY-001
Version: 1.0 (Synthetic)

## Requirements
1. Dormant status must be identified from the account status and inactivity information.
2. Dormant accounts require the applicable review/handling before wallet activation.
3. A dormant account must not be activated during product initialization unless the required handling is documented as completed.

## Audit Evidence
Customer ID, account status, last transaction date, handling status, and wallet status should be available.
